Anatomy & Proportion

Beard Architecture by Face Shape

Discover how our master barbers balance your jawline width, chin projection, and cheekbone prominence.

The Square Jaw

Strong Jawline

Objective: Soften harsh 90-degree corners while elongating the chin to create a more majestic oval balance.

  • Keep cheeks faded short (Grade 1-2).
  • Add density and point graduation to the goatee.
  • Recommended: The Sovereign Tapered Royale.

The Round Profile

Soft Curves

Objective: Create hard vertical angles and razor lines that visually chisel cheekbones and narrow the face.

  • Sharp 45-degree diagonal cheek razor lines.
  • Substantial length under the base of the chin.
  • Recommended: The Chiseled Executive Box.

The Oblong / Long Face

Vertical Height

Objective: Widen the side profiles to add horizontal masculine girth without pulling the chin further downwards.

  • Maintain full volume on sideburns and cheeks.
  • Trim chin hair compact and squared off.
  • Recommended: The Traditional English Full Beard.
