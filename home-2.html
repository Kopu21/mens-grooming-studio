Daily Regimens

The Sovereign 5-Minute Morning Protocol

A refined grooming regimen designed for busy executives to maintain salon sharpness every single day.

Step 1: Botanical Cleansing

Use a sulfate-free beard wash in warm water. Avoid stripping natural oils from facial skin to prevent flaking and split ends.

Step 2: Cold-Pressed Oil Infusion

Warm 4-6 drops of Black Amber & Cedar oil between palms and massage deeply into the roots while beard hair is slightly damp.

Step 3: Boar Bristle Alignment

Brush downwards with firm strokes to train facial hairs, distribute oils uniformly, and remove overnight stray lint.
